# CareerShield Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

CareerShield is a Chrome extension that helps students spot fake job and internship offers.

## What CareerShield does with your data
- **Job pages and files you check:** When you click "Check This Job", or upload a PDF or image of an offer letter, CareerShield reads the text of that page or file **inside your browser** and calculates a risk score. This text is **not sent to any server** and is not stored.
- **Gmail alerts:** On mail.google.com, CareerShield scans the email you have open for scam patterns and may show a warning banner. This also happens **locally in your browser**. Email content is never sent anywhere.
- **Saved on your device:** If you choose to report a sender in Gmail, that sender's address is saved in Chrome's local extension storage on your own device, so the warning is remembered. You can remove it by removing the extension.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
- We do not use analytics, ads, or trackers.
- We do not send page text, emails, files, or browsing history to any server or third party.

## Permissions
- **activeTab / scripting:** to read the job page you choose to check.
- **storage:** to remember reported senders on your device.
- **mail.google.com:** to show warning banners on suspicious job emails.
